# University and Scholarship Links for Algerian Baccalauréat Students (16+/20)

This guide gathers the universities requested, grouped by country, with official university websites and relevant scholarship portals to investigate.

> **Important:** A Baccalauréat average of 16/20 does not automatically guarantee admission or a full scholarship. Funding depends on the scholarship call, nationality eligibility, degree level, age, bac stream, language, and competition. Confirm in writing whether an award covers tuition, accommodation, living expenses, insurance, and travel. Scholarship terms and application deadlines can change each year.

## Checklist before applying

1. Confirm the university offers your intended bachelor's degree and accepts the Algerian Baccalauréat.
2. Confirm that Algerian citizens are eligible for the scholarship and that it supports bachelor's-level study for your intake.
3. Verify coverage for **tuition, accommodation, monthly living support, health insurance, and travel**. Do not interpret “tuition-free” as “fully funded.”
4. Check age limits, bac stream and subject prerequisites, language requirements, exams, and document legalisation.
5. Use official university and scholarship portals and confirm the current deadline.
6. Prepare your bac transcript and diploma, passport, motivation letter, CV if required, recommendations if required, language certificates, and any requested medical documents.

## Practical starting points

Start by checking Türkiye Scholarships, Saudi university scholarship listings, Romania's government scholarship, and the Russian Government Quota. For Hungary, Poland, Malaysia, Germany, and China, confirm that a full bachelor's award is actually available to Algerian applicants before relying on it.
