# Study Tools by Category

Five tools that cover the main steps of studying, from research to exam day.

## 1. Dissertation & Scientific Sources — Elicit
An AI research assistant for your dissertation or thesis. Ask a research question and Elicit finds relevant scientific papers, summarizes them and pulls the key findings (methods, results, sample size…) into a comparison table, which saves hours on a literature review. Free plan with limits; always open and cite the original papers.

- **iOS:** No confirmed dedicated app; use the website.
- **Android:** No confirmed dedicated app; use the website.
- **Website:** https://elicit.com/

## 2. Summarize & Simplify Lessons — NotebookLM
Google's AI notebook (the mobile app is now called **Gemini Notebook**). Upload your course PDFs, slides, Google Docs, websites or YouTube videos, and it answers your questions using only those sources, with citations to the exact passage. It can also generate summaries, study guides, flashcards, quizzes and podcast-style audio overviews of your lessons. Free.

- **iOS:** https://apps.apple.com/app/id6737527615
- **Android:** https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.labs.language.tailwind
- **Website:** https://notebooklm.google.com/

## 3. Presentations — Gamma
Creates a complete, designed presentation from a topic, an outline or your notes in about a minute. You then edit the text, swap images, change the theme and export to PowerPoint or PDF. Free credits to start, paid plans for more.

- **iOS:** https://apps.apple.com/fr/app/gamma-ai-slides-design/id6768404578
- **Android:** https://play.google.com/store/search?q=Gamma%20AI%20Slides%20Design&c=apps
- **Website:** https://gamma.app/

## 4. Practice Exams & Revision — Quizlet
Flashcards and study modes (Learn, Test, Match) to memorize definitions, vocabulary and key facts before an exam. Use millions of ready-made sets or create your own, including from your notes with AI. Free, with extra features in Quizlet Plus.

- **iOS:** https://apps.apple.com/fr/app/quizlet-apprendre-avec-lia/id546473125
- **Android:** https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.quizlet.quizletandroid
- **Website:** https://quizlet.com/

## 5. Solve Mathematical Equations — Wolfram|Alpha
A computational engine that solves equations, derivatives, integrals, limits, matrices and statistics, plus physics and chemistry problems. Type the problem in plain words or math notation; step-by-step solutions require Wolfram|Alpha Pro. Use it to check your answers and understand the method, not to skip practice.

- **iOS:** https://apps.apple.com/fr/app/wolframalpha/id548861535
- **Android:** https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wolfram.android.alpha
- **Website:** https://www.wolframalpha.com/
